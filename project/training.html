Training Tips and Basketball Drills

Use these drills and videos to help improve your basketball skills.

Ball Handling

Follow along with this video to improve your ball handling skills.

Checklist

  • Wide base stance
  • Crossovers (right and left)
  • Double cross
  • Pickups
  • Figure 8 pickups

Shooting

Use this workout to improve your shooting form and consistency.

Checklist

  • Form shooting close to basket
  • Mid-range shots
  • Catch and shoot
  • Corner shots

Rebounding

Learn how to box out and control rebounds on offense and defense.

Checklist

  • Box out your opponent
  • Jump at the highest point
  • Secure the ball
  • Finish with a put back
